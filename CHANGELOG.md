# 0.2.3 · Candidate / 发布候选

Prepared locally; GitHub and npm publication require separate confirmation. / 本地发布准备中，GitHub及npm外发另行确认。

- Arrange rooms beyond the old fixed corridor, with read-only placement checks, bounded position/surface suggestions and final whole-room validation. / 自由布置取消旧固定通道限制，提供只读试摆、有限位置与承载面推荐，仍须最终完整房间校验。
- Furnished forest reading and ceramic work/display layouts, with guidance for adapting registered business materials. / 森林阅读、陶瓷工作陈列成品样板及真实业务资料适配指导。
- All twelve furniture assets support registered-material collections; separate selection from reading, browse all room materials and temporarily hide furniture in overview. / 十二款家具均支持已登记资料集合；选择与阅读分离，完整目录浏览，总览临时隐藏家具。
- Environment sounds, walking footsteps, day/night ambience, rain and wet surfaces; forest ambience fades to silence at rain amount 30. / 环境声、行走脚步、昼夜声音联动、雨天与湿润效果；森林背景声在雨量30时淡出归零。
- Quick brightness and master volume, plus stable display/weather/sound settings. Preferences remain within the current page. / 快捷亮度与总音量，固定显示／天气／声音设置；偏好仅在当前页面保留。
- Updated bilingual guidance and real furnished-room screenshots; corrected outdated furniture descriptions without changing capabilities. / 更新双语指引及真实完整布置截图，仅修正过时家具描述，不改能力。

Existing room, reading and offline-export validation is reused where implementation is unchanged. One successful forest-room AI trial does not establish reliability across models, styles or hosts. / 未变实现复用已有房间、阅读和离线验证；单次森林房AI试用不能代表所有模型、风格或宿主稳定。
